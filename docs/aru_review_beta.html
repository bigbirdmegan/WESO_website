ARU Review: beta testing

This page is for invited beta testers of a desktop app for reviewing acoustic recorder (ARU) detections. If you’ve been invited, enter the password you were given.

THIS IS A BETA VERSION FOR TESTING ONLY. Results are still being checked against pycnet and Kaleidoscope. Please don’t use its outputs for reports, publications or management decisions yet, and please don’t share the download link or password.

ARU Review runs PNW-Cnet (via pycnet-audio) on your recordings, lets you listen to and verify each detection with customizable tags, and summarizes the verified results into Excel, maps and graphs, all in one desktop window with no coding. Everything runs on your own computer; nothing is uploaded.

Download

Getting started

  1. Download the zip (Windows 10 or 11) and unzip it somewhere permanent, such as Documents.
  2. Open Setup guide.html inside it. It walks you through the one-time pycnet install (about 20 minutes) and your first run.
  3. The first time you open ARU Review.exe, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the beta isn’t signed with a paid publisher certificate.

Feedback

Anything that’s confusing, slow, broken or missing is useful, especially results that don’t match what you’d expect. If something goes wrong during a scan, the Copy log button on the Scan tab copies the technical details to paste into an email.
